Quick guide

Drawing

  • Drag on the canvas to paint.
  • Change brush tip, size and rotation.
  • Adjust stroke smoothing and paint load.
  • Choose a paint color or Chrome material.

Gestures

One finger / mouseDraw on canvas
Two fingersUndo last stroke
Three fingersClear entire canvas

Two- and three-finger gestures require a stationary press and release. Movement cancels the gesture.

Other controls

  • Use Undo or Ctrl+Z (⌘Z on Mac) to undo.
  • Use Fullscreen to hide the toolbar.
  • Use the trash button to clear the entire canvas.
